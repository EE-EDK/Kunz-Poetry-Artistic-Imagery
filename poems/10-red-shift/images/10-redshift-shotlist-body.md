# Red Shift — Body Shot List (LOCKED INTRO + 5×5 stills)
Poem 10 · Shadowshining · Jan 29, 2015 · “Red Shift Of Haditha: 322”

## Design rules
- Photoreal documentary weight (Anbar / Albu Nimr / Haditha grounded).
- Faceless figures only when people appear (identities snuffed out).
- No corpses, no graphic violence — remnants, emptiness, symbolic red.
- Each stanza = **one core place/concept** · **5 angles/moments** that flow as a continuous presence.
- Within a stanza: gentle progressive moves (approach → enter → dwell → deepen → leave/turn).
- Between stanzas: soft geographic or emotional handoff (not hard cut of idea).
- After LOCKED intro (~60 s, fades to black) → body starts from black on stanza 1.

## Timing (provisional — refine to mix-FINAL)
Target: each stanza beat cluster ~ equal share of body under voice.
5 stills × ~3–6 s motion each (ping-pong / ambient) → ~15–30 s per stanza before xfades.
Exact lengths = lock to `10-red-shift-mix-FINAL.wav` after first motion pass.

| 1.3 | 10-12 | Along | Traveling-feeling still: road curves; sparse scrub; sky deeper red. |

## STANZA 5 — Pain flows / well runs red
**Core place:** Same well bottom / shaft — symbolic: light turns red; pain as color and atmosphere, not gore.

| # | ID | Beat | Image |
|---|-----|------|--------|
| 5.1 | 10-50 | Glow shifts | Same glow, first tint of red seeping into the white. |
| 5.2 | 10-51 | Well runs red | Shaft walls catch red light; “water” or dust reading as red wash (symbolic). |
| 5.3 | 10-52 | Pain flows | Abstract photoreal: red light climbing the steep sides upward. |
| 5.4 | 10-53 | Town above | Cut-back / dissolve sense: red glow reflected in square/sky (town that’s dead). |
| 5.5 | 10-54 | Close | Final hold: well mouth or shaft closing on deep red → fade toward black or hold for end card. |

**Flow:** tint → flood → rise → return to town → seal.  
**Outro:** soft fade / hold → optional poem title card or silence into end.
